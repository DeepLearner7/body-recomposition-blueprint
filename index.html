CHAPTER 02

The Science of Body Recomposition

"Can you actually build muscle and lose fat at the same time? Peer-reviewed exercise physiology confirms: an unequivocal yes."

⚡ The Energy Flux Equation: How the Body Fuels Muscle Growth

Synthesizing 1 kg of contractile muscle tissue requires approximately 5,000 to 7,500 kcal of metabolic work (peptide bond assembly, ribosome transcription, and cellular remodeling).

At 85 kg, your body possesses tens of thousands of calories stored in adipose reserves. In a mild 12–15% deficit (-300 kcal), your adipose tissue acts as a battery pack, mobilizing fatty acids to cover the deficit and power muscle protein synthesis (MPS).

🧪 The Leucine Threshold (~3.0g per meal)
MPS operates on a binary switch mechanism. Consuming 40g–45g of high-quality protein per meal crosses the 3.0g leucine threshold, activating mTORC1 four distinct times daily.

⚠️ Why Severe Deficits Destroy Recomposition

Crash diets (>500 kcal deficit) activate AMPK, which directly suppresses the mTOR pathway, shuts down muscle growth, collapses circulating testosterone by 15%, and spikes muscle catabolism (MPB). Keep your deficit modest (-300 kcal).

The 4-Day Upper/Lower Hypertrophy Split

"Stimulate, do not annihilate. Muscle growth is triggered by progressive mechanical tension, not extreme soreness."

⚙️ The Double Progression System (1–2 RIR)

Pick a starting weight you can lift for 3 sets of 8 reps. Keep the weight identical across sessions while adding repetitions until you hit 3 sets of 12 reps with pristine form. Then, add +2.5 kg and repeat the cycle.

Proximity to Failure: 1–2 Reps in Reserve (RIR)
Stop every working set with 1 to 2 clean reps left before muscular failure. Never train to sloppy form-breaking failure, which spikes systemic recovery debt without extra hypertrophy.

CHAPTER 11

Exercise Library & Movement Cues

"Form dictates tension; tension dictates growth. Flailing with heavy weights only builds ego and joint wear."

1. Flat Dumbbell Bench Press

Setup: Sit with dumbbells resting on thighs. Kick back. Pinch shoulder blades together like squeezing a pencil between your traps.

Elbow Angle: Keep elbows tucked at 45° to 60° (never 90°). Stop 2 inches short of dumbbells clinking at the top to maintain continuous pectoral tension.

2. Barbell Back Squat & Goblet Squat

Tripod Foot: Distribute weight evenly across your big toe, pinky toe, and heel. Take a diaphragmatic breath into your belly and brace as if about to be punched.

Cue: "Sit down between your hips, not back onto a chair." Hip crease must break parallel with knees.

3. Romanian Deadlift (RDL)

Hip Hinge: Soft bend in knees. Lock that knee angle. Push hips backward as if shutting a car door behind you with your glutes. Lower until hamstrings reach full stretch (mid-shin), then drive hips forward to standing.

4. Lat Pulldown

Scapular Depression: Before elbows bend, depress shoulder blades downwards by 2 inches. Pull bar down to touch upper clavicle while driving elbows down into your back pockets.

5. Standing Overhead Press (OHP)

The Solid Pillar: Squeeze glutes and quads. Brace core. Pull chin back so bar clears nose vertically, then push head through the "window" and lock out with active traps.

6. Cable Lateral Raise

Angle: Lean slightly forward (15°). Push cable out toward the side walls (not up to the ceiling). Imagine pouring water out of a pitcher at the top.
