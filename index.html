⚙️ The Double Progression Rule (1–2 RIR)

Pick a weight you can lift for 3 sets of 8 reps. Keep the weight identical each week until you can execute 3 sets of 12 reps with pristine form. Then, add +2.5 kg to the barbell or grab the next dumbbell. Never train to sloppy failure; stop each set with 1–2 reps in reserve.
