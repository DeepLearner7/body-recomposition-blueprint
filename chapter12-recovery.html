Sleep & Nervous System Recovery

"You do not grow in the gym. You break down muscle fibers in the gym; you rebuild them while sleeping in bed."

🔬 The Hormonal Cost of Sleep Deprivation

When sleep drops below 7 hours per night:

  • Human Growth Hormone (HGH) Collapses: Up to 70% of daily HGH secretion occurs during Stage 3 Slow-Wave Deep Sleep. Sleep deprivation suppresses myofibrillar tissue repair.
  • Testosterone Drops: Sleeping 5 hours per night for a single week reduces circulating testosterone by 10% to 15%.
  • Cortisol & Ghrelin Surge: Elevated cortisol stimulates Muscle Protein Breakdown (MPB), while ghrelin spikes uncontrollable sugar cravings.

🛏️ The 5-Point Sleep Hygiene Protocol

  1. Morning Sunlight Anchor: Get 10–15 mins natural sunlight upon waking to set your internal circadian melatonin timer.
  2. Bedroom Temperature (18°C – 20°C): A cool room allows your core body temperature to drop 1°C, which triggers deep sleep onset.
  3. 60-Minute Digital Sunset: Zero phone screens or blue light 1 hour before bed.
  4. Blackout Darkness: Use blackout curtains or an eye mask to maximize melatonin release.
  5. Warm Shower Vasodilation: A warm shower 45 minutes before bed pulls heat from your core to extremities, accelerating sleep onset.
