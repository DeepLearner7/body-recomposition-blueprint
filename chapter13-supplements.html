⚠️ SEAFOOD ALLERGY MEDICAL ALERT: DO NOT TAKE FISH OIL
Because you are allergic to seafood, standard fish oil and krill oil softgels are strictly dangerous and prohibited due to risks of allergic cross-reaction. Always choose Algal Omega-3 (labeled as vegan/microalgae-derived EPA & DHA, such as Unived, Wellbeing Nutrition Algal, or Tata 1mg Vegan Omega). You get 100% of the anti-inflammatory brain & muscle benefits with zero seafood contact.
🗑️ The Supplement Industry Blacklist (Do Not Waste Money)
  • BCAAs: Totally redundant when you hit 180g whole protein and drink whey.
  • Testosterone Boosters (Tribulus, Ashwagandha blends claiming 500% gains): Zero clinical proof of raising testosterone into supra-physiological ranges in healthy men.
  • Fat Burners / Thermogenics: Cheap caffeine and synephrine that cause jitteriness and elevated heart rate without fat loss.
