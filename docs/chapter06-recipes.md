# Chapter 6: High-Protein Quick Recipes 👨‍🍳

> **"Cooking for body recomposition must be fast, delicious, and repeatable. If it takes 2 hours every night, you won't sustain it."**

Here are 5 core recipes tailored for your 180g protein target. Each takes under 20 minutes to prepare and hits optimal macronutrient ratios.

---

## 🍗 1. Sheet-Pan Lemon Garlic Herb Chicken (Batch Favorite)
* **Prep Time:** 10 mins | **Cook Time:** 20 mins | **Servings:** 4
* **Macros per serving (180g chicken):** 48g Protein | 2g Carbs | 7g Fat | ~270 kcal

### Ingredients
* 750g skinless chicken breasts (cut into cutlets or uniform strips)
* 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
* Juice of 1 whole lemon + zest
* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tbsp dried oregano, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp black pepper, 1 tsp salt

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven or air fryer to 200°C (400°F).
2. In a bowl, toss chicken strips with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and spices.
3. Spread in a single layer on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
4. Bake for 18–20 minutes until internal temperature reaches 74°C (165°F).
5. Divide into 4 glass containers for lunches.

## 🍳 2. The 10-Minute Executive Power Scramble
* **Prep Time:** 3 mins | **Cook Time:** 7 mins | **Servings:** 1
* **Macros:** 39g Protein | 6g Carbs | 20g Fat | ~360 kcal

### Ingredients
* 3 whole large eggs + 2 egg whites (or 60ml liquid egg whites)
* 30g chopped baby spinach
* 1/4 cup diced red onions and bell peppers
* 5g butter or olive oil spray
* Salt, freshly ground pepper, chili flakes to taste

### Instructions
1. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat with butter or oil spray.
2. Sauté onions, bell peppers, and spinach for 2 minutes until wilted.
3. Whisk the whole eggs and whites together with salt and pepper.
4. Pour into the pan, gently folding with a silicone spatula for 3–4 minutes until soft curds form. Serve immediately with whole grain toast or rolled oats.

## 🥣 3. Anabolic Whey & Greek Yogurt Sludge (Dessert or Snack)
* **Prep Time:** 3 mins | **Cook Time:** 0 mins | **Servings:** 1
* **Macros:** 44g Protein | 22g Carbs | 5g Fat | ~310 kcal

### Ingredients
* 170g plain non-fat Greek yogurt (or strained thick curd)
* 1 scoop (30g) Whey Protein Isolate (Chocolate or Vanilla)
* 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder (optional)
* 50g fresh or frozen blueberries
* 1 tbsp water or almond milk (to adjust texture)

### Instructions
1. Add Greek yogurt to a bowl.
2. Pour the whey protein scoop over the yogurt.
3. Slowly stir with a spoon for 60 seconds until completely smooth and pudding-like.
4. Top with berries and a sprinkle of cinnamon.

## 🐟 4. 15-Minute Pan-Seared Salmon or White Fish Fillet
* **Prep Time:** 5 mins | **Cook Time:** 10 mins | **Servings:** 1
* **Macros (with 180g Salmon):** 40g Protein | 0g Carbs | 18g Healthy Fats | ~330 kcal

### Ingredients
* 180g fresh salmon or white fish (cod, tilapia, or basa)
* 1 tsp olive oil
* Pinch of smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper
* Fresh lemon wedges

### Instructions
1. Pat fish fillet dry with paper towels (key to getting a crisp sear).
2. Season both sides with paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
3. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Place fillet skin-side down; cook for 4–5 minutes without moving it.
5. Flip and sear for another 3–4 minutes until flaky. Finish with fresh lemon juice.

## 🥘 5. Quick Soya & Egg High-Protein Stir-Fry (Budget / Quick Option)
* **Prep Time:** 5 mins | **Cook Time:** 10 mins | **Servings:** 1
* **Macros:** 42g Protein | 18g Carbs | 12g Fat | ~350 kcal

### Ingredients
* 40g Soya Chunks (boiled in salted water and squeezed dry)
* 2 Whole Eggs + 2 Egg Whites
* 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 chopped green chili, 1/2 diced onion, 1 chopped tomato
* 1 tsp olive oil, turmeric, and garam masala

### Instructions
1. Sauté cumin, onion, chili, and tomato in olive oil until soft.
2. Add the boiled, squeezed soya chunks and spices. Sauté for 3 minutes.
3. Push soya chunks to the side of the pan, pour beaten eggs into the empty half.
4. Scramble the eggs, then mix everything together. Garnish with fresh cilantro.
