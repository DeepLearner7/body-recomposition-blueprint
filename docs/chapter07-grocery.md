# Chapter 7: The Master Grocery List 🛒

> **"If healthy, high-protein food is in your fridge, you will eat it. If ultra-processed junk is in your pantry, you will eat it. The battle is won in the supermarket."**

Below is your weekly shopping checklist to fuel **180g of protein daily** and sustain your 2,250 / 1,950 kcal calorie targets.

---

## 🥩 Section 1: The Protein Powerhouses (Buy Weekly)

| Item | Weekly Quantity | Primary Purpose / Meal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Boneless Skinless Chicken Breast** | 1.8 – 2.2 kg | Lunches & Dinners (~45g protein per 180g portion) |
| **Fresh Whole Eggs (Free-Range/Omega-3)** | 2 to 3 Dozen (24–36 eggs) | Breakfast scramble (3 whole eggs daily) |
| **Liquid Egg Whites or Extra Eggs** | 1 Cartons (500ml) or extra 12 eggs | Adding pure albumin protein to scrambles without excess fat |
| **Plain Greek Yogurt (0% or 2% Fat)** | 1.0 – 1.5 kg (or thick hung curd) | Nightly high-casein snack / sludge (~18g protein per 170g) |
| **Fresh Salmon Fillets or White Fish** | 400g – 500g | Omega-3 rich dinner protein (2 meals/week) |
| **Whey Protein Isolate Powder** | 1 Tub (1kg - lasts ~4 weeks) | Pre/post-workout shake (30g pure bioavailable protein) |
| **Cottage Cheese / Low-Fat Paneer / Tofu** | 400g – 500g | Quick stir-fry dinner swaps or evening protein |

| Item | Weekly Quantity | Primary Purpose / Meal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rolled Oats (Old Fashioned)** | 1 kg bag | Breakfast complex carbohydrate & beta-glucan fiber |
| **Basmati, Jasmine, or Sona Masoori Rice** | 1 kg bag | Fast-digesting, hypoallergenic workout lunch fuel |

| **Whole Grain Flatbreads / Rotis** | 1 pack or whole wheat flour (Atta) | High-fiber dinner carbohydrate |

---

## 🥑 Section 3: Essential Fats & Micronutrient Greens

### Healthy Fats (Buy Monthly / Bi-Weekly)
* **Extra Virgin Olive Oil (Cold-Pressed):** 1 Bottle (primary cooking/dressing fat).
* **Raw Almonds & Walnuts:** 250g bag (vitamin E, magnesium, healthy monounsaturated fats).
* **Natural Peanut / Almond Butter:** 1 Jar (ingredients should just be peanuts and salt).
* **Chia Seeds or Ground Flaxseeds:** 200g bag (soluble fiber and alpha-linolenic acid).

### Vegetables (Buy Fresh Twice a Week or Large Weekly Haul)
* **Broccoli Crowns or Green Beans:** 800g (cruciferous sulforaphane, high volume, low calories).
* **Baby Spinach:** 2 large boxes/clamshells (nitrates for blood flow, magnesium, lutein).
* **Bell Peppers (Red, Yellow, Green):** 4 to 6 peppers (vitamin C for connective tissue).
* **Cucumbers & Tomatoes:** 1 kg each (high water volume for garden salads).
* **Fresh Lemons & Garlic:** 6 lemons, 2 heads of garlic (natural flavor enhancers & digestive aid).

---

## 🧂 Section 4: Condiments & Zero-Calorie Flavor Enhancers

* **Pink Himalayan Salt or Sea Salt:** Crucial for workout pumps and hydration balance.
* **Black Peppercorns & Garlic Powder.**
* **Smoked Paprika, Cumin, Garam Masala, Italian Herbs.**
* **Sriracha, Tabasco, or Mexican Hot Sauces:** 0 calories, adds bold flavor to dry chicken.
* **Dijon or Whole Grain Mustard:** Low-calorie condiment for marinades.
* **Cinnamon Powder:** Enhances insulin sensitivity; great on morning oats.
