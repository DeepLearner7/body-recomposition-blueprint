# Chapter 3: Caloric Balance & TDEE Calculations ⚖️

> **"Energy balance dictates your body mass trend; macronutrient partition dictates whether that mass is muscle or fat."**

To optimize body recomposition at **184 cm and 85 kg**, you must have an accurate, mathematically grounded caloric foundation. Guessing or using arbitrary numbers from social media will lead to wasted weeks.

---

## 🧮 Step 1: Calculating Your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)

Your **Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)** represents the energy expended by your vital organs (brain, liver, heart, kidneys, lungs) just to keep you alive in a comatose, fasted state.

Using the gold-standard **Mifflin-St Jeor Equation** for a male at 184 cm and 85 kg (assuming age ~30):

$$\text{BMR} = (10 \times \text{weight in kg}) + (6.25 \times \text{height in cm}) - (5 \times \text{age}) + 5$$

$$\text{BMR} = (10 \times 85) + (6.25 \times 184) - (5 \times 30) + 5$$

$$\text{BMR} = 850 + 1150 - 150 + 5 = \mathbf{1,855 \text{ kcal/day}}$$

---

## 🏃‍♂️ Step 2: Factoring in Activity (TDEE)

Your **Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)** includes your BMR plus:
1. **TEF (Thermic Effect of Food):** Energy required to digest protein, carbs, and fat (~10% of total intake).
2. **EAT (Exercise Activity Thermogenesis):** Energy burned during your 4 gym sessions (~250–350 kcal per session).
3. **NEAT (Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis):** Walking, taking stairs, posture, daily movement (8,000–10,000 daily steps).

For a 4-day commercial gym lifting schedule paired with an 8,000–10,000 daily step count, your standard **Physical Activity Level (PAL) multiplier is 1.35 to 1.40**:

$$\text{TDEE (Maintenance)} = 1,855 \times 1.375 \approx \mathbf{2,550 \text{ kcal/day}}$$

---

## 🎯 Step 3: Calibrating the Recomposition Calorie Intake

In a traditional "cut," coaches prescribe a 20–25% deficit (-500 to -700 kcal). But severe deficits impair muscle protein synthesis. 

For recomp, we target a **mild 12% to 15% deficit (~300 to 350 kcal below maintenance)**:

$$\text{Average Daily Intake} = 2,550 - 325 = \mathbf{2,225 \text{ kcal/day}}$$

## 💧 Hydration & Intracellular Water

Muscle tissue is **75% water** by weight. If you are even 2–3% dehydrated:
* Muscular strength output drops by 10–15%.
* Cellular swelling (a known driver of muscle protein synthesis) is diminished.
* Fat oxidation slows down.

### Your Hydration Protocol
* **Daily Fluid Volume:** **3.5 to 4.0 Litres** of plain water.
* **Pre-Workout Electrolyte Boost:** Add 1/4 teaspoon (approx 1g) of pink Himalayan rock salt or sea salt to your pre-workout water bottle 30 minutes before training. Sodium increases blood volume, improves vasodilation, and maximizes gym pumps.
