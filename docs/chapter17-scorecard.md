# Chapter 17: The Weekly Audit Scorecard 📋

> **"What gets measured gets managed. You cannot improve what you do not audit."**

Complete this scorecard every Sunday evening during your meal prep window. It removes emotional guesswork and gives you a cold, objective score out of **100 Points** for your week.

---

## 💯 The 100-Point Recomposition Adherence Matrix

### 🥩 Section 1: Nutrition & Fueling (35 Points Max)
* [ ] **180g Protein Standard:** Consumed 175g–185g protein every day.
  *(Award 3 points per day: **21 Points Max**)*
* [ ] **Calorie Target Discipline:** Stayed within ±75 kcal of daily target (2,250 on training days / 1,950 on rest days).
  *(Award 2 points per day: **14 Points Max**)*

### 🏋️‍♂️ Section 2: Training & Progressive Tension (30 Points Max)
* [ ] **Workout Execution:** Completed all 4 scheduled sessions (Upper A, Lower A, Upper B, Lower B).
  *(Award 5 points per completed workout: **20 Points Max**)*
* [ ] **Logbook Tracking:** Every single exercise weight and rep was recorded in your journal/app.
  *(Award **5 Points**)*
* [ ] **Proximity to Failure (RIR):** Maintained strict 1–2 RIR with pristine form, avoiding sloppy failure.
  *(Award **5 Points**)*

### 🚶‍♂️ Section 3: NEAT & Hydration (15 Points Max)
* [ ] **Daily Step Benchmark:** Hit between 8,000 and 10,000 steps daily.
  *(Award 1.5 points per day: **10 Points Max**)*
* [ ] **Hydration Floor:** Drank 3.5 to 4.0 Litres of water daily.
  *(Award **5 Points**)*

### 🌙 Section 4: Recovery & Supplements (20 Points Max)
* [ ] **Sleep Target:** Achieved 7.5 to 8.5 hours of sleep in a cool, dark room.
  *(Award 2 points per night: **14 Points Max**)*
* [ ] **Supplement Adherence:** Took 5g Creatine, D3/K2, Omega-3, and Magnesium daily.
  *(Award **6 Points**)*

---

## 🏆 Adherence Grade & Action Triggers

$$\text{Weekly Total Score} = \text{Nutrition} + \text{Training} + \text{NEAT/Water} + \text{Recovery}$$

| Total Score | Execution Grade | Expected Biological Result | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **90 – 100** | **Elite / Optimal** | Rapid simultaneous fat loss and muscle hypertrophy. | Maintain exact protocol. Do not change anything. |
| **80 – 89** | **Solid Progress** | Steady progress; slight friction in sleep or weekend diet. | Identify the 1–2 minor leaks (e.g., weekend steps or late bedtime). |
| **70 – 79** | **Marginal / Stagnant** | Body recomposition will be sluggish and inconsistent. | Re-read Chapter 8 (Meal Prep) and Chapter 9 (Office Strategy). |
| **Below 70** | **Failing Systems** | Scale will fluctuate; zero muscle growth; high frustration. | Stop and reset. Simplify to hitting just 180g protein and 4 gym days. |
