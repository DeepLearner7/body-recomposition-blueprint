# Chapter 15: Troubleshooting & FAQ ❓

> **"Obstacles will arise. The difference between those who transform their bodies and those who quit is having a pre-determined protocol for every obstacle."**

---

### Q1: I am suffering from severe muscle soreness (DOMS) for 3 days. Should I train through it?
**Answer:** Severe Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness (DOMS) is completely normal during your first 2 to 3 weeks as your muscle fibers adapt to novel eccentric loading. 
* **If soreness is 3/10 or below:** Warm up thoroughly and train. Increased blood flow will actually reduce the soreness (*repeated bout effect*).
* **If soreness is debilitating (7/10 or above):** Take an extra active recovery day (go for a brisk 45-minute walk and foam roll). Never train through sharp joint or tendon pain. Within 3 weeks, severe DOMS will cease almost entirely.

---

### Q2: What if I miss a workout due to an unavoidable work meeting?
**Answer:** Never try to "cram" two workouts into one 3-hour marathon session. 
* Simply shift your schedule forward by one day. 
* For example, if you miss Tuesday's Lower A session, perform Lower A on Wednesday (your active rest day), and proceed with Upper B on Thursday as usual. The human body operates on weekly rolling volume, not strict 24-hour calendar boxes.

---

### Q3: Can I drink alcohol on weekends during a recomp?
**Answer:** Biologically, alcohol significantly blunts body recomposition:
1. **Shuts Down Fat Oxidation:** When alcohol enters the bloodstream, the liver prioritizes oxidizing acetate (the toxic breakdown product of alcohol), reducing systemic fat burning by over **70% for up to 24 hours**.
2. **Suppresses MPS:** High alcohol intake blunts Muscle Protein Synthesis by up to 37% even when protein is consumed.
* **The 85 kg Guideline:** If you choose to drink, limit it to **1–2 standard drinks maximum** (e.g., dry wine, clear spirits with club soda and lime). Avoid sugary cocktails, beers, and late-night drunk snacking, which will instantly erase your weekly deficit.

---

### Q4: I feel hungry around 4:00 PM at the office. How do I fix this?
**Answer:** Late afternoon hunger is typically driven by two factors: low hydration or an insulin dip from lunch.
1. **Drink 500ml cold water** first; thirst is frequently misperceived as hunger.
2. Ensure your lunch had at least **150g of fibrous green vegetables** and **45g of protein**.
3. If hunger persists, have your scheduled **Meal 3 snack early** (a scoop of whey with 15g almonds, or 150g Greek yogurt).

---

### Q5: My scale weight hasn't moved in 3 weeks. Am I stuck?
**Answer:** Before changing your calories, check your secondary metrics:
1. **Has your navel waist measurement decreased by even 0.5 cm?** If YES, you are losing fat and building muscle at identical rates. Keep going!
2. **Are your gym lift numbers increasing in your logbook?** If YES, you are gaining pure muscle tissue.
3. **If BOTH waist and scale are completely flat AND your gym lifts are stalled for 3 consecutive weeks:** Reduce daily calories by **150 kcal** (cut 35g of carbs from your rest days) and increase daily steps by 1,500.

---

### Q6: Should I take Whey Protein and Creatine on rest days?
**Answer:** **Yes, absolutely.**
* Muscle recovery and cellular protein synthesis continue for 24 to 48 hours after your training sessions. Your muscles grow on your rest days!
* **Creatine** must be taken every single day (5g) to maintain full intracellular muscle saturation.
