# Chapter 5: The 4-Meal Daily Blueprint 🍽️

> **"A diet you cannot follow consistently for 6 months is useless. Structure and simplicity create freedom."**

Below is your exact, gram-for-gram omnivore daily meal template calibrated for **184 cm and 85 kg**, hitting **180g of protein**.

---

## ⚡ Training Day Template (~2,250 kcal | 180g P | 220g C | 70g F)

### 🍳 Meal 1: Breakfast Power Bowl (approx. 8:00 AM)
* **3 Whole Eggs + 2 Large Egg Whites** (scrambled or poached in 5g butter/ghee).
* **60g Rolled Oats** (cooked in water or unsweetened almond milk) topped with **100g blueberries/berries** and a pinch of cinnamon.
* **15g Raw Almonds or Walnuts**.
* **Macros:** ~530 kcal | **38g Protein** | 50g Carbs | 22g Fats

### 🥗 Meal 2: High-Performance Work Lunch (approx. 1:00 PM)
* **180g Grilled Chicken Breast** (weighed raw / ~140g cooked).
* **180g Cooked Basmati or Jasmine Rice** (~60g raw).
* **150g Steamed Broccoli, Green Beans, or Zucchini**.
* **10g Extra Virgin Olive Oil** drizzled over vegetables.
* **Macros:** ~580 kcal | **46g Protein** | 55g Carbs | 18g Fats

### 🥤 Meal 3: Peri-Workout Anabolic Shake (approx. 5:30 PM - 6:00 PM)
* **1.25 Scoops (approx. 38g) Whey Protein Isolate** (yielding ~30g pure protein).
* **250ml Skim Milk or Soy Milk** (~9g protein).
* **1 Medium Banana** (approx. 110g) for rapid glycogen transport.
* **Macros:** ~360 kcal | **41g Protein** | 42g Carbs | 3g Fats

### 🍗 Meal 4: Nightly Recovery Dinner (approx. 8:30 PM)
* **170g Lean Protein:** Choice of Chicken Breast, Salmon Fillet, or Grilled Fish (or 150g Paneer/Tofu stir-fry).
* **200g Boiled or Roasted Sweet Potatoes** (or 2 Medium Whole Wheat Rotis / Flatbreads).
* **Large Fresh Garden Salad:** Cucumbers, tomatoes, bell peppers, mixed greens with lemon juice and salt.
* **10g Olive oil or flaxseed oil** for dressing.
* **Macros:** ~570 kcal | **45g Protein** | 52g Carbs | 19g Fats

---

## 🛑 Rest Day Adjustments (~1,950 kcal | 180g P | 150g C | 65g F)

On non-training days (Wednesday, Saturday, Sunday), your energy expenditure is lower. We drop carbohydrates by ~70g while keeping protein rock solid at 180g:

* **Meal 1:** Keep eggs identical; drop oats from 60g to 35g.
* **Meal 2:** Reduce cooked rice from 180g to 120g; double the green vegetables.
* **Meal 3 (Snack):** Replace the banana with a scoop of whey isolate mixed into **150g Low-Fat Greek Yogurt (or hung curd)** with cinnamon.
* **Meal 4:** Replace sweet potatoes with extra leafy greens, asparagus, and a slightly larger lean protein portion.
* **Result:** **1,950 kcal | 180g Protein | 150g Carbs | 65g Fats**.
