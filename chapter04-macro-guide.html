CHAPTER 04 • MACRONUTRIENT ARCHITECTURE

The 180g Macro Protocol

"Protein builds the structure. Carbohydrates power the performance. Fats orchestrate the endocrine system."

🥩 Pillar 1: Protein (The Structural Foundation)

At 85 kg, your daily target is 180 Grams (~2.1 g/kg bodyweight). Spreading this across 4 feedings of ~45g hits the leucine threshold 4 times per day:

  • Anti-Catabolic Shield: Protects muscle tissue during energy deficit.
  • Thermic Effect of Food (TEF): Digesting 180g protein burns ~180 kcal daily.

🥑 Pillar 2: Healthy Fats (The Endocrine Floor)

Your target is 65g – 70g / day (~0.75 – 0.8 g/kg). Dropping below 50g crashes testosterone synthesis in Leydig cells and impairs joint lubrication.

🍚 Pillar 3: Carbohydrates (Performance & Glycogen)

Target: 220g on Training Days / 150g on Rest Days. Concentrate 65% of carbs in the peri-workout window (pre-workout snack and post-workout meal).
