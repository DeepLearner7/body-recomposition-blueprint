🌿 Rest Day Protocol (1,950 kcal | Wed, Sat, Sun)

On rest days, energy expenditure is lower. Maintain protein strictly at 180g, but reduce carbohydrate portions:

  • Meal 1: Keep eggs identical; drop rotis from 2 to 1 (or oats from 60g to 35g).
  • Meal 2: Reduce cooked rice from 170g to 110g; double the green salad and cucumber.
  • Meal 3: Swap the banana for 150g hung curd (Shrikhand recipe from Ch 6) with whey.
  • Meal 4: Swap sweet potatoes for sautéed green vegetables (shimla mirch, French beans, gobi).
