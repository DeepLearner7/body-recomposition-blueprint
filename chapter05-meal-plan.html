CHAPTER 05 • 7-DAY MEAL BLUEPRINT

The Complete 7-Day Indian Recomposition Meal Plan

"A diet is only as good as your ability to stick to it. Here is your full Monday-to-Sunday roadmap hitting exactly 180g protein every day with calorie cycling. Every single meal links directly to an easy step-by-step recipe in Chapter 6."
Day Type Days of Week Calories Protein Target Carbohydrates Fats Metabolic Goal
Training Days Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri 2,250 kcal 180g 220g 70g Fuel heavy progressive overload & maximize muscle protein synthesis
Rest Days Wed, Sat, Sun 1,950 kcal 180g 150g 65g Lower carbohydrates to accelerate fat oxidation while protecting lean mass
💡 Beginner Tip for Weekly Consistency
You do not need to cook 28 separate meals every week! Notice how lunch and dinner recipes repeat strategically (e.g. Kasuri Methi Chicken Tikka, Egg Bhurji, Soya Paneer Curry). Pre-boil eggs and pre-chop onions/tomatoes for 10 minutes on Sunday to make weekday cooking effortless.
