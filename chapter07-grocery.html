🍳 The Beginner Cook's 6-Piece Hardware Arsenal

You do not need an expensive chef's kitchen. To cook every meal in this blueprint in under 15 minutes, you only need these 6 basic tools:

⚖️ 1. Digital Food Scale (₹350–₹500)

The single most critical purchase for body recomposition. Eliminates guesswork. Weighs your chicken raw (220g), rice cooked (160g), and almonds (15g) accurately.

🍳 2. Heavy 28cm Non-Stick Tawa

Allows searing chicken tikka, frying fish, and cooking besan cheelas with strictly 1 level teaspoon of oil without sticking or tearing.

🍲 3. Deep Kadhai with Glass Lid (2–3L)

Essential for egg bhurji, soya chunks curry, and palak chicken. The glass lid traps moisture so chicken breasts steam to juicy perfection.

💨 4. 2–3L Pressure Cooker

Your weekday speed secret: turns tari chicken, moong dal khichdi, and rajma pulao into foolproof 2-whistle, 12-minute meals.

🥄 5. Standard Measuring Spoons (5ml / 15ml)

Prevents the fatal 200 kcal oil leak. 1 measured level teaspoon is exactly 5ml (45 kcal). Never free-pour directly from a bottle!

🌪️ 6. Smoothie Blender or Shaker Bottle

Blends banana-whey shakes and cold coffee in 30 seconds with zero chalky lumps. Keep a blender cup dedicated to your post-workout smoothies.
