CHAPTER 07 • SUPPLY CHAIN LOGISTICS

The Master Indian Grocery List

"If healthy, high-protein food is in your fridge, you will eat it. The battle is won in your weekly supermarket order."

🥩 1. Complete Proteins (Buy Weekly via Zepto / Local Market)

ItemWeekly QuantityHousehold Notes
Skinless Chicken Breast2.0 – 2.2 kgAsk butcher for boneless breast fillets or order online.
Farm Fresh Whole Eggs2 to 3 Dozen (24–36 eggs)Daily morning scramble and emergency boiled snacks.
Low-Fat Paneer / Tofu400g – 500gAmul or Mother Dairy low-fat paneer (100g = ~18g protein).
Soya Chunks (Nutrela / Fortune)1 Pack (200g–500g)Cost-effective plant protein (52g protein per 100g dry).
Fresh Fish (Rohu / Surmai / Basa)400g – 500gCleaned fillets for bi-weekly omega-3 dinners.
Greek Yogurt / Thick Curd (Dahi)1.0 – 1.5 kgEpigamia Greek Yogurt or hung homemade curd.
Whey Protein Isolate Powder1 kg Tub (Monthly)Pre/post-workout shake (e.g., Optimum Nutrition, MuscleBlaze).

🍚 2. Clean Performance Carbohydrates

  • Whole Wheat Flour (Chakki Atta): 5 kg bag (Aashirvaad / local mill).
  • Basmati or Sona Masoori Rice: 2 kg bag. Fast-digesting, hypoallergenic workout lunch fuel.
  • Rolled Oats (Jav): 1 kg bag (True Elements / Quaker Rolled Oats).
  • Sweet Potatoes (Shakarkandi): 1.5 kg from local mandi. High potassium for muscle contractility.
  • Fresh Bananas: 7 bananas (pre-workout rapid glucose).

🥑 3. Healthy Fats, Spices & Sabzi Mandi Greens

  • Cold-Pressed Mustard Oil & Desi Ghee: Measured strictly with teaspoons.
  • Raw Badam (Almonds) & Walnuts (Akhrot): 250g bag.
  • Vegetables (Buy Twice Weekly): 1 kg Palak (spinach), 500g Shimla Mirch (capsicum), 1 kg Tomatoes, 1 kg Red Onions, 1 kg Cucumbers, 8 Lemons, Ginger & Garlic.
