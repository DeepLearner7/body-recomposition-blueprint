CHAPTER 06 • CULINARY BLUEPRINT

High-Protein Indian Household Recipes

"The greatest hurdle in Indian fitness is hidden fats and low protein density. These 8 recipes are engineered for self-cooking in a standard Indian kitchen using basic cookware (tawa, kadai, mixer)."
⚠️ The 3 Indian Kitchen Recomp Commandments
  1. Measure Oil with a Teaspoon (Never Free Pour): 1 tablespoon of oil poured directly from a bottle can easily add 15ml (135 kcal). 1 teaspoon measured is exactly 5ml (45 kcal). That 90 kcal difference 3 times a day erases your entire deficit!
  2. Separate Egg Yolks from Whites: 3 whole eggs provide bioavailable cholesterol, choline, and vitamins; adding 3 egg whites doubles the protein (+11g) with only 50 extra calories and 0g fat.
  3. Squeeze Soya Chunks Completely: After boiling soya chunks in salted water, rinse in cold water and squeeze out all excess moisture with your hands before cooking so they absorb spices like meat.

1. Desi Masala Egg Bhurji with Whole Wheat Phulkas

Traditional spicy street-style scramble optimized for 42g protein
Prep: 5 Mins Cook: 8 Mins Cookware: Non-Stick Tawa / Kadai
Calories
520 kcal
Protein
42.0 g
Carbs
46.0 g
Fats
18.5 g
Fiber
7.0 g
Leucine
3.5 g

🛒 Raw Ingredients

  • Whole Farm Eggs 3 Large
  • Egg Whites (separated) 3 Large (90g)
  • Whole Wheat Flour (Atta) 60g (Makes 2 Phulkas)
  • Finely Diced Red Onion 1 Small (50g)
  • Finely Chopped Tomato 1 Medium (60g)
  • Green Chili & Ginger 1 chili + 1/2 inch minced
  • Mustard Oil or Desi Ghee 1 tsp (5ml / 5g)
  • Spices (Haldi, Jeera, Pav Bhaji Masala, Salt) To taste
  • Fresh Coriander (Dhaniya) Handful chopped

🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method

1
Whisk 3 whole eggs and 3 egg whites together in a bowl with a pinch of salt and turmeric until frothy.
2
Heat 1 tsp oil/ghee in a non-stick kadai over medium flame. Add 1/2 tsp jeera and let it crackle for 20 seconds.
3
Add chopped onions, green chili, and minced ginger. Sauté for 3 minutes until onions turn translucent pink.
4
Add chopped tomatoes, 1/2 tsp haldi, 1/2 tsp red chili, and 1/2 tsp pav bhaji masala. Cook for 2 minutes until tomatoes soften.
5
Lower flame and pour the whisked eggs. Let sit undisturbed for 15 seconds, then fold gently with a spatula for 2–3 minutes until soft, fluffy curds form. Garnish with dhaniya. Serve immediately with 2 dry tawa phulkas.

2. Tawa Grilled Kasuri Methi Chicken Tikka Bowl

Juicy tandoori-spiced chicken breast paired with fragrant jeera rice
Prep: 15 Mins Cook: 12 Mins Cookware: Heavy Non-Stick Tawa
Calories
590 kcal
Protein
54.0 g
Carbs
55.0 g
Fats
16.0 g
Fiber
6.0 g
Leucine
4.8 g

🛒 Raw Ingredients

  • Boneless Chicken Breast (Cubed) 220g (Raw weight)
  • Hung Curd (Dahi strained in cloth) 50g
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste 1 tbsp (15g)
  • Kashmiri Red Chili + Tandoori Masala 1 tsp each
  • Kasuri Methi (Crushed) 1 tsp
  • Mustard Oil 1 tsp (5ml) for smoky flavor
  • Lemon Juice 1 tbsp
  • Cooked Basmati Rice 160g (~55g raw)
  • Kachumber Salad (Cucumber, Onion, Tomato) 150g

🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method

1
In a bowl, mix hung curd, ginger-garlic paste, mustard oil, Kashmiri red chili powder, tandoori masala, crushed kasuri methi, lemon juice, and salt.
2
Coat the 220g chicken breast cubes thoroughly. Marinate for 15 minutes (or prep overnight in the fridge).
3
Heat a heavy non-stick tawa or grill pan over medium-high heat. Spray or brush lightly with water/oil.
4
Place chicken cubes without crowding. Sear undisturbed for 5 minutes until char marks form. Flip and cook for another 4–5 minutes until cooked through.
5
Plate hot alongside 160g cooked jeera rice and a large bowl of crunchy kachumber salad with chaat masala.

3. Kadhai Soya Chunks & Low-Fat Paneer High-Protein Curry

The ultimate plant-and-dairy protein combo with 48g complete protein
Prep: 10 Mins Cook: 12 Mins Cookware: Iron or Non-Stick Kadai
Calories
510 kcal
Protein
48.0 g
Carbs
38.0 g
Fats
18.0 g
Fiber
11.0 g
Leucine
3.6 g

🛒 Raw Ingredients

  • Soya Chunks (Nutrela / Fortune) 50g (Raw dry weight)
  • Low-Fat Paneer 100g (Diced into cubes)
  • Green Bell Pepper (Capsicum) 1 Medium (Diced)
  • Onion & Tomato Puree 1 onion + 2 tomatoes blended
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste 1 tsp (5g)
  • Cooking Oil / Ghee 1 tsp (5ml)
  • Spices (Coriander, Cumin, Garam Masala, Kasuri Methi) 1 tsp each
  • Accompaniment 1 Whole Wheat Roti or 100g Rice

🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method

1
Boil 50g soya chunks in 3 cups of water with 1/2 tsp salt for 5 minutes. Drain, rinse under cold water, and squeeze out all excess liquid firmly with your hands.
2
Heat 1 tsp oil in a kadai. Add 1/2 tsp jeera, followed by diced capsicum. Sauté on high flame for 2 minutes to keep them crunchy. Remove capsicum and set aside.
3
In the same kadai, add ginger-garlic paste and the onion-tomato puree. Cook for 4–5 minutes until oil starts separating from the masala.
4
Add haldi, red chili, coriander powder, and garam masala. Toss in the squeezed soya chunks and 100g low-fat paneer cubes. Add 1/2 cup water.
5
Cover and simmer for 4 minutes so the paneer and soya absorb the spices. Mix in the sautéed capsicum and crushed kasuri methi. Serve with 1 warm roti.

4. High-Protein Palak Chicken Saag / Keema Bowl

Tender chicken breast simmered in iron-rich, velvety garlic-spinach puree
Prep: 10 Mins Cook: 15 Mins Cookware: Mixer Grinder & Kadai
Calories
540 kcal
Protein
51.0 g
Carbs
48.0 g
Fats
15.0 g
Fiber
8.0 g
Leucine
4.4 g

🛒 Raw Ingredients

  • Chicken Breast (Minced or Diced) 200g (Raw weight)
  • Fresh Spinach (Palak) Leaves 200g (Cleaned & washed)
  • Garlic Cloves (Lehsun) 6 cloves (Finely minced)
  • Green Chilies 2 (For kick)
  • Onion 1 Medium (Finely diced)
  • Desi Ghee / Olive Oil 1 tsp (5g)
  • Garam Masala & Cumin Seeds 1/2 tsp each
  • Steamed Rice or Rotis 150g Rice or 2 Phulkas

🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method

1
Blanch 200g palak leaves in boiling water for 90 seconds. Transfer immediately to cold water (preserves vivid green color). Blend in mixer with green chilies into a smooth puree.
2
Heat 1 tsp ghee in a pan. Add jeera and minced garlic. Sauté until garlic turns golden aromatic.
3
Add chopped onions and sauté for 3 minutes. Add 200g diced or minced chicken breast, salt, and black pepper. Stir-fry on medium flame for 5 minutes until chicken turns white and releases juices.
4
Pour the palak puree over the chicken. Add 1/2 tsp garam masala. Simmer on low heat for 5 minutes until gravy thickens and chicken is completely tender.
5
Squeeze fresh lemon juice on top. Serve with 150g steamed basmati rice or 2 rotis.

5. Pan-Tawa Masala Fish Fry (Rohu / Surmai / Basa) with Lemon Rice

High-potency Omega-3 fish steaks with coastal mustard-turmeric crust
Prep: 10 Mins Cook: 10 Mins Cookware: Flat Tawa
Calories
530 kcal
Protein
46.0 g
Carbs
52.0 g
Fats
14.0 g
Fiber
4.0 g
Leucine
3.9 g

🛒 Raw Ingredients

  • Fresh Fish Fillets (Rohu / Basa / Surmai) 220g
  • Turmeric (Haldi) + Red Chili Powder 1/2 tsp + 1 tsp
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste 1 tsp (5g)
  • Lemon Juice 1 tbsp
  • Mustard Oil or Coconut Oil 1 tsp (5ml)
  • Cooked Rice with Lemon & Curry Leaves 160g
  • Cucumber Tomato Salad 100g

🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method

1
Pat fish fillets completely dry with kitchen paper. Make small shallow diagonal incisions on both sides.
2
Make a paste of haldi, red chili powder, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, salt, and 1/2 tsp oil. Rub all over fish. Rest for 10 minutes.
3
Heat a flat non-stick tawa over medium-high heat with the remaining 1/2 tsp oil.
4
Place fish gently. Sear for 4 minutes without moving to develop a crisp spiced crust. Flip and cook for 3–4 minutes until fish flakes easily.
5
Serve hot with 160g lemon-tempered rice and fresh sliced onions and lemons.

6. Anabolic Kesar-Elaichi Dahi & Whey Protein Shrikhand

Sweet craving crusher with 46g slow-digesting casein + whey protein
Prep: 3 Mins Cook: 0 Mins (No Cooking) Cookware: Mixing Bowl & Spoon
Calories
360 kcal
Protein
46.0 g
Carbs
22.0 g
Fats
9.0 g
Fiber
4.0 g
Leucine
4.2 g

🛒 Raw Ingredients

  • Thick Hung Curd (Chakka / Greek Yogurt) 200g (Plain, unsweetened)
  • Whey Protein Isolate (Vanilla / Kulfi / Mango) 1 Scoop (30g powder = 26g protein)
  • Green Cardamom (Elaichi) Powder 1/4 tsp freshly ground
  • Saffron (Kesar) Strands 4–5 strands soaked in 1 tsp warm milk
  • Raw Almonds (Badam) Sliced 15g (~10–12 almonds)
  • Chia Seeds 1 tsp (5g)
  • Pomegranate (Anar) Seeds 30g for natural crunch

🍳 Step-by-Step Cooking Method

1
Take 200g hung curd (made by hanging regular dahi in a muslin cloth for 2 hours to drain whey liquid) in a bowl.
2
Add 1 scoop whey protein isolate, cardamom powder, and the soaked kesar milk.
3
Using a spoon or whisk, beat vigorously for 60 seconds until transformed into an ultra-thick, creamy Shrikhand pudding.
4
Top with slivered badam, chia seeds, and fresh anar seeds. Chill in the freezer for 10 minutes before eating. Perfect bedtime muscle-building dessert.
