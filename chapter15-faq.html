CHAPTER 15 • TROUBLESHOOTING

Frequently Asked Questions & Obstacles

"The difference between those who transform their bodies and those who quit is having a pre-determined protocol for every friction point."

Q1: I am suffering from severe muscle soreness (DOMS) for 3 days. Should I train?

Answer: Severe Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness (DOMS) is completely normal during your first 2 to 3 weeks. If soreness is 3/10 or below, warm up thoroughly and train (increased blood flow accelerates repair). If debilitating (7/10 or above), take an active walking rest day. Within 3 weeks, severe DOMS ceases completely.

Q2: Can I drink alcohol on weekends during a recomp?

Answer: Alcohol prioritizes acetate metabolism in the liver, suppressing systemic fat burning by over 70% for up to 24 hours and blunting muscle protein synthesis. Limit to 1–2 standard drinks maximum (dry red wine or clear spirits with soda and lime). Avoid sugary cocktails and late-night drunk snacking.

Q3: What if I miss a gym session due to a late work meeting?

Answer: Never try to "cram" two workouts into one marathon 3-hour session. Shift your schedule forward by one day. If you miss Tuesday's Lower A session, perform Lower A on Wednesday (your active recovery day) and resume Upper B on Thursday. The body operates on rolling weekly stimulus.

Q4: My weight hasn't moved in 3 weeks. Am I stuck?

Answer: Before dropping calories, check your secondary metrics. Has your navel waist measurement decreased by even 0.5 cm? Are your gym lift weights or reps increasing? If YES, you are gaining muscle and losing fat at equal rates. Only reduce calories (by -150 kcal) if both waist and scale are completely stalled for 3 consecutive weeks.
