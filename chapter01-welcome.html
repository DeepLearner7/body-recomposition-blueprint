Welcome & The Blueprint Roadmap

"You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems." — James Clear

🧭 Your Starting Baseline: 184 cm & 85 kg

At 184 cm (6'0.5") and 85 kg (187 lbs), your Body Mass Index (BMI) is approximately 25.1. Because you are just starting out with structured resistance training, you hold the ultimate biological advantage: Untrained Muscle Plasticity.

⚡ The Novice Window Unlocked
Untrained muscle fibers have never experienced high mechanical tension. When exposed to structured progressive resistance training, your rate of Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS) spikes higher and remains elevated significantly longer (48–72 hours) than in elite lifters. Your body can readily draw from your adipose fat reserves to fuel this muscle-building process.

🗺️ The 12-Week Recomposition Roadmap

  • Phase 1: Neuromuscular Adaptation (Weeks 1–4): Mastering compound movement patterns (squat, hinge, press, pull). Building mind-muscle connection. Locking in 180g daily protein. Rapid neural strength jumps with zero fat gain.
  • Phase 2: Progressive Hypertrophy & Fat Mobilization (Weeks 5–8): Strict double progression (+2.5 kg or +1 rep per set). Calorie cycling execution (2,250 on lifting days / 1,950 on rest days). Visible reduction in navel waist measurement and broader shoulders.
  • Phase 3: Muscle Density & Consolidation (Weeks 9–12): Peak working set intensity with 1–2 RIR. Automated lifestyle habits. Visible abdominal definition and structural posture improvements.

🧠 The 3 Mindset Principles

  1. The Gym Logbook is Truth: If your scale weight stays at 85 kg over 8 weeks, but your bench press and squat increase steadily, you have gained pure contractile muscle and burned significant body fat.
  2. Protein is an Appointment, Not an Afterthought: Space 180g protein across 4 structured meals. You cannot make up 100g of protein late at night.
  3. Consistency Beats Intensity: Four focused 50-minute sessions executed every single week for 12 weeks will transform your body far more than sporadic extreme routines.
