CHAPTER 16 • SUSTAINABLE LIFESTYLE

Long-Term Maintenance & Reverse Dieting

"Fitness is not an event you finish; it is a permanent operating system you install."

🔄 The Reverse Dieting Protocol

After 12 to 16 weeks of body recomposition, your metabolism will have adapted to the lower energy intake. If you suddenly jump from 2,100 kcal back to unmeasured eating, your fat cells will rapidly absorb excess calories.

To transition safely, add +100 to +150 kcal per day (+30g carbs) every 2 weeks until you reach your true maintenance level (~2,550 kcal). This restores thyroid T3, increases gym strength, and maintains zero fat rebound.

🛡️ Minimum Effective Volume (MEV) Lifesaver

If high-intensity workplace stress or travel arises, remember: Maintaining 100% of existing muscle requires only 3 to 5 hard working sets per muscle group per week. Switching to a 2-day full body maintenance routine will preserve all your gains until life settles down.

⚖️ The 80/20 Lifestyle Rule

  • 80% of Intake: Single-ingredient whole foods (eggs, chicken, low-fat paneer, soya chunks, dahi, rice, oats, fresh sabzi, mustard/olive oil).
  • 20% Flexibility: Family dinners, social weddings, and dining with friends.
