CHAPTER 08 • KITCHEN EFFICIENCY

The 90-Minute Sunday Meal Prep Workflow

"You do not need to spend your entire weekend in the kitchen. 90 minutes of parallel batch cooking on Sunday afternoon guarantees your nutrition for the entire work week."

🛠️ Essential Equipment for Indian Home Cooking

  • Digital Kitchen Food Scale: Non-negotiable for measuring 200g chicken or 60g raw rice.
  • 4 Glass Lunch Containers (Borosil / Treo): Glass keeps dabba food fresh and reheats without microplastics.
  • Heavy Flat Tawa or Air Fryer / Oven: For batch cooking chicken tikka or fish.
  • Rice Cooker or Pressure Cooker: For zero-effort carbohydrate batching.

🔄 The 90-Minute Parallel Workflow

  1. Minute 0–15 (Marination & Rice): Cube 1 kg chicken breasts. Toss with hung curd, ginger-garlic paste, kasuri methi, tandoori masala, lemon, and 1 tsp mustard oil. Measure 350g rice into cooker; start cycle.
  2. Minute 15–40 (Tawa Sear / Baking): Pan-sear chicken on tawa or bake on a sheet-pan at 200°C for 20 mins. Boil 8 eggs in a small pot for quick afternoon snacks.
  3. Minute 40–60 (Resting & Veggies): Rest chicken for 10 minutes so juices don't evaporate. Steam 600g broccoli, beans, or carrots for 4 minutes.
  4. Minute 60–75 (Assembly): Zero glass containers on the food scale. Fill each with 180g cooked chicken tikka, 170g cooked rice, and 120g veggies. Seal airtight.
  5. Minute 75–90 (Clean Up): Wash pans, label containers, and place Mon–Wed containers in fridge and Thu–Fri in freezer.
💡 The Microwave Reheat Trick for Office Dabba
When reheating chicken breast in an office microwave, cover the container with a damp paper towel and heat at 70% power for 2 minutes. This traps steam and keeps the chicken tender and juicy instead of dry.
