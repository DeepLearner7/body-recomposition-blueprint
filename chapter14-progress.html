CHAPTER 14 • AUDIT & METRICS

Measuring True Progress (The 3-Metric Rule)

"If you only measure bodyweight on the bathroom scale, you will think your body recomposition is failing when it is actually succeeding."

📐 The Scale Paradox at 85 kg

In 6 weeks of recomp, you may lose 2.0 kg of visceral and subcutaneous fat while gaining 1.8 kg of dense muscle and intracellular water from creatine. The bathroom scale reads 84.8 kg (an imperceptible change of -0.2 kg). To the uninformed, it feels like a plateau. In reality, your body has experienced a radical structural transformation.

🎯 The 3-Metric Rule

  • 1. Navel Waist Circumference: Measure with a tape across your belly button on Sunday morning after voiding, before food/water. Target: -0.5 to -1.0 cm per month.
  • 2. Gym Logbook Numbers: If your bench press and squat are steadily gaining reps or weight within the 8–12 bracket, you are unequivocally gaining contractile myofibrillar muscle tissue.
  • 3. 7-Day Scale Moving Average: Weigh in every morning unclothed; average the 7 days. This filters out daily sodium, water, and digestive transit fluctuations.
