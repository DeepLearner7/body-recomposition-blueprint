CHAPTER 03 • ENERGY BALANCE

Caloric Balance & TDEE Calculations

"Energy balance dictates your body mass trend; macronutrient partition dictates whether that mass is muscle or fat."

🧮 Step 1: Calculating Your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)

Using the gold-standard Mifflin-St Jeor Equation for a male at 184 cm and 85 kg (age ~30):

BMR = (10 × 85) + (6.25 × 184) - (5 × 30) + 5 = 850 + 1150 - 150 + 5 = 1,855 kcal/day

🏃‍♂️ Step 2: Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)

For a 4-day gym lifting schedule combined with an 8,000–10,000 daily step count, your Physical Activity Level (PAL) multiplier is 1.375:

TDEE (Maintenance) = 1,855 × 1.375 ≈ 2,550 kcal/day

🎯 Step 3: Calorie Cycling Protocol

Instead of eating a flat number every day, we cycle energy intake to match metabolic demand:

  • Training Days (4 Days / Wk): 2,250 – 2,300 kcal (fuel gym performance, maximize glycogen and intracellular muscle pump).
  • Rest Days (3 Days / Wk): 1,950 – 2,000 kcal (maximize fat oxidation and insulin sensitivity).
  • Weekly Deficit: -2,925 kcal/week ≈ -0.38 kg fat lost per week (~1.5 kg/month).
💧 Intracellular Hydration Standard
Drink 3.5 to 4.0 Litres of plain water daily. Add 1/4 tsp pink Himalayan salt to your pre-workout bottle 30 minutes before training to optimize vascular pump and muscle contractility.
